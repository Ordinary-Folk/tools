The OF look

How every Ordinary Folk tool looks and behaves: principles, colour, type, spacing and the house icons. Change things here first; the apps take their icons and tokens from this page's files.

Principles

Colour

One set of names, two themes. Light for the Tracker, Schedule, Time Tracker and this hub; dark for OF Review. Greys run lightest to darkest: line for borders and hover fills, line strong for strong borders and faint text, muted for metadata, text 2 for body copy, text for headings and key values.

Palette

Every colour in the OF tools, grouped by colour, lightest to darkest. Under each: the tokens and tools that use it (d = dark theme). Built from tokens.css, so it never drifts.

OF Ordinary Folk brand colour, named after Lorena's Explorations V3 (outlined swatch).

Geometry

Sharp corners everywhere. Round only for avatars, status dots and toggles. Spacing on a 4px scale, laid out with gap, not margins.

Controls 36px tall (28px in toolbars), fields 40px, taps at least 44px on phones. Two shadows: popover and overlay. No glows, no coloured shadows.

Icons

All house icons, Pixelarticons Pro plus OF custom drawings. One name per idea, used the same way in every app.

  • Whole pixels only: 12px next to text, 24px for big targets. Never 16px or 1em.
  • Outline by default; Solid where it reads better or means "done".
  • Colour comes from the text. Logos, charts and people's emoji stay as they are.
  • Directions never share an icon; boxed arrows for moves, plain chevrons for menus.

How apps use this

1. House listlook/icons/house.json names every icon (search, settings, resolve…) and what it draws.
2. App listEach app's pixel-icons.json points at house names: "search": "@search".
3. Generatornode look/icons/gen.mjs <app> writes the app's icon file. --all-apps does every app.
4. ShipCommit and deploy the app. Change a house icon once, re-run, every app follows.

Colours, type and spacing come from tokens.css (same names in light and dark). New icons: draw them in the maker, save the SVG into look/icons/custom/, add a house name.
